About this plan

Eight weeks, four sessions a week, no equipment. Two strength days to hold onto muscle while you cut, one conditioning circuit, one full-body and core day. Rounds and difficulty step up every couple of weeks — week 7 uses harder variations of the same movements.

Work at a pace you can repeat. Soreness in the first fortnight is normal; sharp joint pain is not — swap that exercise or stop. If you have a heart condition, an injury, or you have been inactive a long while, get a doctor's blessing before starting.

Weigh in

Weigh yourself at the same time each day — first thing in the morning, after the bathroom, before eating. Consistency matters far more than the exact number.
